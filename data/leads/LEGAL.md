# Legal — leads only

CEO (2026-09-05): aggressive multi-source collection is **approved for lead gathering**, stored here with provenance. That is not approval to publish, to copy third-party databases into the live catalog, or to bypass access controls.

## Risk

Scraping or bulk-copying aggregator and tourism databases is **high ToS and EU database-right (sui generis) risk**. Lists like Camping Ireland, Pitchup, and county tourism pages are typically protected as collections even when individual facts (a name, a phone) are not copyrightable.

## Rules

- Store **leads with provenance only**. This folder is Research, not the published directory.
- Do **not** copy copyrighted prose, reviews, or photos into fields that will be published.
- Do **not** document or implement authentication or CAPTCHA bypass.
- **Counsel before publish** (or before any bulk import that would make aggregator copy public). Curators promote original or licensed descriptions, not scraped blurbs.
- Personal mailboxes stay out of `email_public`.

Engineering import (`PRD-002`) reads this store as input. It does not change these constraints.
