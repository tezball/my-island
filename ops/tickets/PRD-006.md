# PRD-006 — Research leads store (data/leads)

## Outcome

`data/leads/` is the source of truth for place **leads** (campsites, B&Bs, experiences, POIs) collected from the web. Leads are **not** published places. Research fills JSONL; Engineering later imports into Spring; curators promote. Seed by migrating `docs/leads/CAMPSITE_LEADS.md`.

## Notes

CEO 2026-09-05: aggressive multi-source scrape is approved for **lead collection only**, stored with provenance. No live catalog writes. Feeds [[tickets/PRD-002]] (curator import) — this ticket does **not** implement Spring import or scrapers.

Owner: **content-seo** (Research). Do not treat `docs/leads/` as the living list.
