# PRD-008 — Curator promote-from-lead in admin

## Outcome

After [[tickets/PRD-001]] Place catalog skeleton, a curator can **promote a lead** from [`data/leads/places.jsonl`](../../data/leads/places.jsonl) into a Spring Place. The Place keeps the lead’s **`source_url`**, **`source_name`**, and **`licence`** from [`data/leads/schema.json`](../../data/leads/schema.json). Curator can reject or skip. **No auto-publish.** Not booking.

## Notes

Lead record shape is [`data/leads/schema.json`](../../data/leads/schema.json) (PR #14). Do not invent a second lead DTO. Map curator actions onto the **landed** `status` enum only:

| Curator action | Lead `status` | Catalog |
|---|---|---|
| Skip | unchanged (`lead` or `reviewed`) | no Place |
| Reject | `rejected` | no Place |
| Review facts | `reviewed` | still unpublished |
| Import to draft Place | stay `reviewed` | unpublished Place; copy `source_url`, `source_name`, `licence` |
| Publish Place | `promoted` | published Place — only after explicit curator publish **and** [[tickets/PRD-009]] when the batch is scraped/aggregated |

README: do not skip to `promoted` from the JSONL store alone. `promoted` means imported **and** published.

Owner: **eng-backend**. **content-seo** operates the queue. Relates to [[tickets/PRD-002]] (spreadsheet bulk-import / coverage — separate). Pipeline bar: [[tickets/PRD-007]].

**Explicitly not in this ticket:** booking, payments, inventory, partner portals, reviews, messaging, auto-publish from scrape, Next/FastAPI, a new leads schema.

## Verify
