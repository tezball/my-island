# PRD-008 — Curator promote-from-lead in admin

## Outcome

After [[tickets/PRD-001]] Place catalog skeleton, a curator can **promote a lead** from [`data/leads/`](../../data/leads/README.md) to a **draft or published Place** in Spring. Source + licence are preserved. Curator can reject or skip. **No auto-publish.** Not booking.

## Notes

Content path: Research deposits leads ([[tickets/PRD-006]], [[tickets/PRD-007]]) → curator promotes here → catalog. Relates to [[tickets/PRD-002]] (bulk create/import/coverage). This ticket is the **lead → Place** admin action (CUR-06 / CUR-09), not spreadsheet bulk-import and **not** marketplace booking ([[tickets/PRD-004]]).

Owner: **eng-backend** (admin/API). **content-seo** operates the queue. Counsel gate [[tickets/PRD-009]] must be recorded before any bulk publish from leads. Leads stay unpublished until a human curator promotes. Rejected leads stay in JSONL for dedupe (`status: rejected`). Draft is allowed; published requires curator intent plus counsel when the batch is scraped/aggregated.

**Explicitly not in this ticket:** booking, payments, inventory, partner portals, reviews, messaging, auto-publish from scrape, Next/FastAPI.
