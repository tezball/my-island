# Plan: Research leads store (data/leads)

## Outcome

A git-tracked leads store at `data/leads/` with JSON Schema, seeded JSONL from the ~29 campsites in `docs/leads/CAMPSITE_LEADS.md`, Research workflow docs, and a pointer from the old table. Done when a PR has schema + seed + README.

## Out of scope

Scrapers, Spring/Flyway import, curator admin UI, writing the published catalog, editing [`product/SIGNED.md`](../../product/SIGNED.md), resurrecting Next/Neon.

## Steps

1. Add `data/leads/schema.json` for one lead (id, place_type, provenance, status lifecycle).
2. Convert every row in `docs/leads/CAMPSITE_LEADS.md` to `places.jsonl` (`status=lead`; omit personal emails).
3. Write `README.md` (purpose, lifecycle lead → reviewed → rejected → promoted, rules).
4. Add `SOURCES.md` + `LEGAL.md` (ToS / database-right risk; leads-only; counsel before publish; no auth/CAPTCHA bypass).
5. Leave a pointer in `docs/leads/` and a short note on [[tickets/PRD-002]] that Research fills `data/leads/`.
6. Pytest: JSONL parses, unique ids/dedupe keys, schema shape, 29 campsite leads.
