How do I register my campsite for the upcoming season?

MI
Written by My Island Support Last updated 2 days ago

To register your campsite, first ensure you are logged into your supplier account. Once logged in, you can manage all your properties directly from the dashboard.

Step-by-step Guide

  • 1

    Navigate to the 'My Properties' tab located in the bottom navigation bar.

  • 2

    Tap the add icon in the top right corner to start a new application.

  • 3

    You will be prompted to upload proof of ownership and valid safety permits.

info

Approval Process

Most applications are reviewed within 24-48 hours. You will receive a push notification once your campsite is live.
