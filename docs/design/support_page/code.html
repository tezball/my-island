What is a valid registration? expand_more
A valid campsite registration requires a verified supplier account, clear photos of the site, and accurate GPS coordinates. Ensure all safety amenities are listed.
Turn off weather alerts? expand_more
Go to your profile settings, select 'Notifications', and toggle the 'Severe Weather' switch. We recommend keeping this on for safety during active bookings.
How to contact a supplier? expand_more
Once a booking is confirmed, a chat button will appear in your 'My Trips' tab allowing direct communication with the campsite owner.
