About this place

Experience the rugged beauty of West Cork at Eagle's Point. Located directly on the Wild Atlantic Way, our site offers panoramic views of Bantry Bay. Whether you're pitching a tent or parking an RV, you'll wake up to the sound of waves and fresh sea air.
