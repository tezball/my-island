## Revenue Models Comparison

> [!abstract] Two Revenue Approaches
> This report compares two distinct monetization strategies for the platform.

### Model A: Commission-Based (Industry Standard)

| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Platform Fee** | 10% of booking value |
| **Paid By** | Deducted from owner payout OR added to guest total |
| **Owner Receives** | 90% of listed price |

### Model B: Subscription + Flat Fee (Proposed)

| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Property Subscription** | €20/month per campsite |
| **Booking Fee** | €3 per booking |
| **Paid By** | Subscription by owner, booking fee by visitor |
| **Owner Receives** | 100% of listed price |

### Break-Even Analysis

> [!question] When does Subscription Model match Commission Model?
> The subscription model generates ~10% of commission revenue. To match, you would need:

| To Match Commission Revenue | Required Change |
|----------------------------|-----------------|
| Increase subscription to €200/month | 10x subscription |
| Increase booking fee to €30 | 10x booking fee |
| Hybrid: €50/month + €10/booking | ~3x each component |

## Model Trade-Off Analysis

### Model A: Commission (10%)

| Pros | Cons |
|------|------|
| ✅ Higher revenue per transaction | ❌ Owners lose 10% of earnings |
| ✅ Revenue scales with booking value | ❌ May deter premium properties |
| ✅ Industry-standard model | ❌ Complex payout calculations |
| ✅ Still undercuts competitors (15-20%) | ❌ Revenue drops if avg price falls |

### Model B: Subscription + Flat Fee

| Pros | Cons |
|------|------|
| ✅ Owners keep 100% of their price | ❌ ~90% less revenue than commission |
| ✅ Predictable recurring revenue | ❌ Fixed income regardless of booking value |
| ✅ Lower barrier for owner sign-up | ❌ €3 fee same for €22 tent vs €185 cottage |
| ✅ Simple, transparent pricing | ❌ Subscription may deter small operators |
| ✅ Aligns incentives (platform wants more bookings) | ❌ Need 10x more campsites to match revenue |

## Hybrid Model Option

> [!example] Alternative: Tiered Subscription + Percentage Fee
> A middle-ground approach to capture benefits of both models.

### Proposed Hybrid Structure

| Tier | Subscription | Booking Fee | Best For |
|------|-------------|-------------|----------|
| **Starter** | €20/month | €3 flat | Small sites, 1-3 lots |
| **Professional** | €50/month | 5% of booking | Medium sites, 4-10 lots |
| **Enterprise** | €100/month | 3% of booking | Large parks, 10+ lots |

### Hybrid Revenue Projection (500 Campsites, 60% occupancy)

Assuming distribution: 60% Starter, 30% Professional, 10% Enterprise

| Component | Annual Revenue |
|-----------|---------------|
| Starter (300 sites) | €72,000 subscriptions + €121,239 fees = €193,239 |
| Professional (150 sites) | €90,000 subscriptions + €481,950 fees = €571,950 |
| Enterprise (50 sites) | €60,000 subscriptions + €192,780 fees = €252,780 |
| **TOTAL** | **€1,017,969** |

*This hybrid generates 32% of pure commission model, but 316% of pure subscription model.*

## Seasonality Adjustment

> [!warning] Seasonal Impact
> Irish camping is highly seasonal - revenue varies significantly throughout the year.

| Season | Months | Occupancy | Commission Impact | Subscription Impact |
|--------|--------|-----------|-------------------|---------------------|
| Peak | Jun-Aug | 85% | Revenue increases | Bookings increase |
| Shoulder | Apr-May, Sep-Oct | 60% | Baseline | Baseline |
| Off-Peak | Nov-Mar | 25% | Revenue drops | Subscriptions continue |

### Seasonally-Adjusted Annual Revenue (500 campsites)

| Model | Peak (3mo) | Shoulder (4mo) | Off-Peak (5mo) | Annual Total |

| **Subscription+Fee** | €120,774 | €107,355 | €134,701* | €362,830 |

*Off-peak subscription model includes full €10K/month subscriptions even with low booking volume - a key advantage.*
