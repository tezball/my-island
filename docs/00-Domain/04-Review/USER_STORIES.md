# Review User Stories

## Guest Stories

- As a Guest, I want to write a review after my stay to share my experience.
- As a Guest, I want to rate specific aspects (cleanliness, location, etc.) to give detailed feedback.
- As a Guest, I want to read reviews from other travelers to help me decide where to book.

## Owner Stories

- As an Owner, I want to respond to reviews to address feedback.
- As an Owner, I want to see my average rating to understand performance.
