# Review User Stories

> **Status: Implemented** — Campsite reviews, supplier reviews, owner/supplier responses, review eligibility enforcement, admin moderation, and post-stay review emails are all live.

## Guest Stories (Implemented)

- As a Guest, I want to write a review after my completed stay so I can share my experience.
- As a Guest, I want to rate specific aspects (star rating + text) to give detailed feedback.
- As a Guest, I want to read reviews from other travelers on campsite detail pages to help me decide where to book.
- As a Guest, I want to review a supplier after redeeming an offer so other guests can benefit from my experience.
- As a Guest, I want the system to enforce review eligibility (completed booking or redeemed offer required) so reviews are trustworthy.

## Owner Stories (Implemented)

- As an Owner, I want to respond to guest reviews from my Reviews page to address feedback.
- As an Owner, I want to see my aggregate rating displayed on my campsite card and detail page to understand performance.

## Supplier Stories (Implemented)

- As a Supplier, I want to view customer reviews on my Supplier Reviews page.
- As a Supplier, I want to respond to customer reviews to address feedback.

## Admin Stories (Implemented)

- As an Admin, I want to flag/unflag inappropriate reviews from the admin Review Moderation page.
- As an Admin, I want to search and filter reviews to find problematic content.

## Automated Engagement (Implemented)

- As a Platform, I want to send a post-stay review request email 1 day after checkout to encourage guest reviews. (Daily scheduler at 10 AM, ShedLock protected, skips bookings with existing review or already-sent email.)

## Planned (Not Yet Built)

- As a Guest, I want to edit my review within a grace period if I made a mistake.
- As an Owner, I want to receive a notification when a new review is posted.
