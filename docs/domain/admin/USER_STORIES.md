# Admin User Stories

> **Status: Implemented** — Full platform admin portal with 15 pages covering dashboards, management, moderation, financials, CRM, and audit logging.

## Dashboard (Implemented)

- As an Admin, I want to see a dashboard with KPIs (total users, bookings, revenue, MRR) so I can monitor platform health.
- As an Admin, I want to see an activity feed of recent platform events.
- As an Admin, I want to see booking breakdown and revenue trend charts for visual analytics.

## User Management (Implemented)

- As an Admin, I want to search and filter users by role, status, and name.
- As an Admin, I want to view user details including their bookings, reviews, and account status.
- As an Admin, I want to activate or deactivate user accounts.

## Booking Management (Implemented)

- As an Admin, I want to search and filter bookings by status, date range, and property.
- As an Admin, I want to view booking details and status analytics across the platform.

## Owner Management (Implemented)

- As an Admin, I want to search for and view owner details and their properties.
- As an Admin, I want to verify owners and see their subscription status.

## Supplier Management (Implemented)

- As an Admin, I want to search for and view supplier details and their offers.
- As an Admin, I want to verify suppliers and see their subscription status.

## Review Moderation (Implemented)

- As an Admin, I want to flag inappropriate reviews to hide them from public view.
- As an Admin, I want to unflag reviews that were incorrectly flagged.
- As an Admin, I want to search and filter reviews to find problematic content.

## Subscription Overview (Implemented)

- As an Admin, I want to see active, trial, and cancelled subscription counts with revenue breakdown.

## Financial Reporting (Implemented)

- As an Admin, I want to view revenue reports including MRR, ARR, and payout summaries.
- As an Admin, I want to export financial data as CSV for external analysis.

## Lead CRM (Implemented)

- As an Admin, I want to track prospective owners and suppliers as leads with status and scoring.
- As an Admin, I want to log interactions with leads (calls, emails, meetings, notes).
- As an Admin, I want to schedule follow-ups and track lead conversion.

## Audit Log (Implemented)

- As an Admin, I want a complete audit trail of all admin actions with entity snapshots (before/after state).
- As an Admin, I want to search and filter the audit log by action type, entity, and date range.

## Planned (Not Yet Built)

- As an Admin, I want to send bulk communications to owners/suppliers.
- As an Admin, I want to manage platform-wide settings (commission rates, trial duration).
