# Frequently Asked Questions

## Accommodation Types

### What types of accommodation can I list on My Island?

My Island supports **5 accommodation types** designed for the Irish camping and glamping market:

| Type | Best For | What's Included |
|------|----------|-----------------|
| **Tent Pitches** | Traditional campers who bring their own tent | Designated pitch area, access to shared facilities (toilets, showers, washing up) |
| **Touring Pitches** | Guests with caravans, campervans, or motorhomes | Hardstanding or grass pitch, electric hookup, may include water/waste connections |
| **Glamping** | Guests seeking luxury camping without the setup | Pre-pitched accommodation (bell tents, yurts, pods, safari tents) with beds and furnishings |
| **Cabins & Lodges** | Families and couples wanting solid walls | Wooden cabins or lodges with heating, may include private bathroom and kitchenette |
| **Mobile Homes** | Guests wanting a home-from-home experience | Static caravans with full kitchen, bathroom, living area, and bedrooms |

### Why only 5 types? Other platforms have more options.

We've designed our categories specifically for the Irish market:

1. **Simplicity** - Guests can quickly find what they're looking for without wading through 15+ categories
2. **Accuracy** - Irish sites typically don't distinguish between caravan vs campervan pitches - they're all "touring pitches"
3. **Discoverability** - Glamping covers all luxury camping (yurts, pods, bell tents) because guests search for the *experience*, not the structure type
4. **Relevance** - Mobile homes are huge in Ireland but often missing from camping platforms

### What falls under "Glamping"?

Glamping includes any pre-pitched, premium camping accommodation:
- Bell tents
- Yurts
- Safari tents
- Camping pods
- Geodesic domes
- Shepherd's huts (if tent-like experience)
- Any other "ready to stay" luxury camping unit

### What's the difference between a Cabin and a Lodge?

For listing purposes, they're the same category. Generally:
- **Cabin** - Smaller, cosier, 1-2 bedrooms
- **Lodge** - Larger, more amenities, 2+ bedrooms

Both are wooden/permanent structures. List whichever term best describes your accommodation.

### Can I list multiple accommodation types at my site?

Yes! Most campsites offer a mix. For example:
- 20 tent pitches
- 10 touring pitches
- 5 glamping pods
- 3 cabins

Each lot type is listed separately with its own pricing and availability.

## For Owners

### How do I register my campsite?

1. Create an account or sign in
2. Click "Become a Host" from your profile
3. Follow the onboarding steps:
   - Property details (name, location, description)
   - Select accommodation types you offer
   - Configure lots and pricing
   - Add photos
   - Set up payment (subscription)
4. Your listing goes live after review

### Can I offer both camping and glamping?

Absolutely. Select multiple accommodation types during onboarding and configure each one separately.

### How is pricing set?

You set a **price per night** for each lot type. You can also:
- Offer extras (electric hookup, firewood, bike rental)
- Set seasonal pricing (coming soon)
- Create special offers

## For Guests

### How do I search for accommodation?

1. Enter your destination (county, town, or "near me")
2. Select check-in and check-out dates
3. Browse results or filter by accommodation type
4. Click on a listing to see available lots and book

### Can I filter by accommodation type?

Yes! On the search results page, you can filter by:
- Tent Pitches
- Touring Pitches
- Glamping
- Cabins & Lodges
- Mobile Homes

### What's included in my booking?

Each listing shows:
- What's included (e.g., electric hookup, bedding)
- Shared facilities available (showers, toilets, WiFi)
- Optional extras you can add
- Check-in/check-out times
- House rules

## Technical

### Why can't I find [specific accommodation type]?

We've consolidated accommodation types into 5 main categories. If you're looking for:
- **Yurt, pod, bell tent** → Search "Glamping"
- **Caravan, campervan, motorhome pitch** → Search "Touring Pitches"
- **Treehouse, shepherd's hut** → Search "Cabins & Lodges" or "Glamping" depending on the setup
- **Static caravan** → Search "Mobile Homes"

### I'm an owner with a unique accommodation type. How do I list it?

Choose the category that best matches the guest experience:
- If guests bring their own shelter → **Tent Pitches** or **Touring Pitches**
- If it's a pre-set luxury experience → **Glamping**
- If it's a permanent structure with walls → **Cabins & Lodges**
- If it's a static caravan/mobile home → **Mobile Homes**

Contact support if you're unsure - we're happy to help!
