# Mission

People planning time on the island of Ireland, Republic and Northern Ireland, can find a place to stay, see what is nearby, and book the things they want to do. Hosts run their own listings. An admin keeps the public site clean.

The product has three kinds of listing:

- **Stay.** A campsite, glamping, or bed and breakfast. A host has many listings. One page per listing, and that page can list many lots or rooms.
- **Place.** A point of interest, such as Powerscourt Waterfall or the Cliffs of Moher. A signed-in person can check off a place they have been.
- **Experience.** Something you do, such as renting a bike, a kayak, or a boat trip.

The app is aware of where the person is. Later it can point at a deal, a place, or an experience nearby. The MVP does not notify anyone. It lists, lets a host manage their listings, and lets a person check off places they have been.

Money is not taken in the app. A later booking is approved by the host, and the guest pays the host in person. That booking loop is not the MVP.

Android and iOS share the product later through Kotlin Multiplatform. The MVP is the web app, built phone-first.
