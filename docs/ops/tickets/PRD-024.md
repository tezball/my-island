# PRD-024 — Stay reviews after checkout

## Outcome

After a Booking reaches **checked_out** (or checkout date passed), the Guest may post one **Review** (rating + text). Shown on the Stay page without Guest PII beyond display name. Host may **respond once**. Flag for moderation ([[ops/tickets/PRD-026]]).

## Notes

Depends on [[ops/tickets/PRD-019]]. Reviews are **not** VisitIntent. POI pages do not gain reviews in this ticket. Automated screening can be a stub queue.

## Verify

- [ ] Cannot review before checkout; one review per Booking
- [ ] Stay page shows aggregate + list; no email/ids
- [ ] Host cannot hide a review; can respond once
- [ ] Contract tests; moderation flag lands in admin queue
- [ ] No Next.js BFF
