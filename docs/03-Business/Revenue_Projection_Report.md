# my-island Revenue Projection Report

> [!info] Report Overview
> Financial projections for the my-island booking platform based on the Irish accommodation market and a subscription + booking fee revenue model.

---

## Revenue Model

| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Property Subscription** | €20/month per property |
| **Booking Fee** | €3 per booking (paid by visitor) |
| **Owner Receives** | 100% of their listed price |

## Seasonality Impact

> [!warning] Irish Accommodation Seasonality
> Revenue from booking fees fluctuates with seasons, but subscription revenue remains constant.

| Season | Months | Occupancy | Booking Revenue Impact |
|--------|--------|-----------|----------------------|
| Peak | Jun-Aug | 85% | +42% above baseline |
| Shoulder | Apr-May, Sep-Oct | 60% | Baseline |
| Off-Peak | Nov-Mar | 25% | -58% below baseline |

## Summary

### Ireland-Only Revenue Potential

| Market Capture | Properties | Annual Revenue (60% occ) |
|----------------|------------|-------------------------|
| Outdoor only | 250 | €161K |
| + 20% B&Bs | 500 | €322K |

### Revenue Range by Occupancy

| Properties | Conservative (40%) | Realistic (60%) | Optimistic (80%) |
|------------|-------------------|-----------------|------------------|
| 250 | €127K | €161K | €195K |
| 500 | €255K | €322K | €389K |
| 1,000 | €509K | €644K | €779K |
| 1,500 | €764K | €966K | €1.17M |

> [!abstract] Key Takeaway
> With the subscription + booking fee model (€20/mo + €3/booking), capturing the full Irish market of ~1,500 properties generates approximately **€1M annually** at realistic occupancy. To exceed €1M ARR in Ireland alone requires either higher occupancy, increased fees, or expansion to UK/EU markets.
